TV power consumption

How much energy does a TV use?

These four charts explore TV energy use and electricity prices. Start with the scatter plot to see how star rating relates to energy use, then compare screen technologies and the trend in electricity prices over time.

Star rating vs energy consumption

Each dot is a group of TV models with the same brand, screen technology, size and star rating, coloured by screen technology. Higher star ratings tend to use less energy.

Electricity price over time

Spot price per megawatt hour by year for each state (coloured lines) and the national average (black line).

Mean energy use by screen technology

Average annual consumption (kWh/year) for each technology. Switch between all sizes and 55-inch TVs.

Share of TVs by screen technology

Proportion of models in the data set for each technology.
